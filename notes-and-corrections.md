# Notes Checking & Corrections (Sample)

## Purpose
Keep student work accurate and give feedback they can act on.

## Process
1. Collect notebooks or worksheets on the agreed day.
2. Check for completeness, accuracy and neat presentation.
3. Mark errors and write a short correction or hint.
4. Ask the student to redo the corrections.
5. Re-check and record the outcome.
6. Flag repeated mistakes to the tutor and parents.

## Feedback examples
**Weak:** "Wrong. Do again."
**Strong:** "Step 3 has a sign error. Recheck the subtraction, then redo
questions 4 and 5."
