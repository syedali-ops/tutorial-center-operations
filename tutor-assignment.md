# Tutor Assignment (Sample)

## Purpose
Match tutors to classes and subjects so every batch is covered and nothing clashes.

## Steps
1. List classes, subjects and time slots for the term.
2. List each tutor's subjects, strengths and available hours.
3. Match by subject first, then availability, then workload.
4. Check for clashes (same tutor, two batches, same slot).
5. Share the timetable with tutors and confirm.
6. Review monthly and adjust based on student results.

## Sample timetable (fictional)
| Class | Subject | Tutor | Day / Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Maths | Tutor A | Mon, Wed 5:00 PM |
| 8 | Science | Tutor B | Tue, Thu 5:00 PM |
| 10 | English | Tutor C | Mon, Fri 6:00 PM |

## Checklist
- [ ] Every batch has a tutor
- [ ] No time clashes
- [ ] Workload is balanced
- [ ] Tutors confirmed the schedule
